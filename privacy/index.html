Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

JPG Studio (Zahle, Lebanon) makes short videos for businesses and runs the Instagram account @jpgstudio.lb. This policy explains what we collect when you message us on Instagram or email us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram username and the ID Instagram gives us for your account.
  • The messages you send us, and any photos, videos or voice notes you choose to send.
  • Business details you tell us (business name, type, city) so we can make your video.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages, including automatic replies written by an AI model that runs on our own computer. Your messages are not sent to any outside AI company.
  • To make the videos you ask for. Your photos and videos are only used in your own videos, and never posted on our account without your permission.
  • To follow up on an order you started.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. We do not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

On our own computer in Lebanon, plus Meta's servers as part of the normal Instagram conversation. We keep it while we are talking with you or working for you, and delete it within 12 months of our last contact, or earlier if you ask.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you or delete everything we hold about you at any time. See Data deletion, DM us "DELETE", or email test2jpgservices@gmail.com.
